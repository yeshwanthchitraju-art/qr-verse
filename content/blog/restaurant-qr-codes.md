QR codes went from a novelty to a necessity in restaurants almost overnight. But the best operators aren't just using them for menus — they're turning them into a growth channel.

## 1. Digital menus that update instantly

Printed menus are expensive and slow to update. A dynamic QR code on the table lets guests scan to view your menu on their phone. When a dish sells out or a price changes, you update the page — not the QR.

## 2. One-tap reservations

Link your QR to your reservation system. Guests scanning from the waiting area can book a table without asking the host. Fewer abandoned waits, more covers.

## 3. Collect reviews while it's fresh

The best time to ask for a review is right after the meal. Point your QR at a review collection page. Guests leave feedback while they're still at the table — when the experience is fresh.

## 4. Loyalty signups

A QR code on the receipt or check presenter can link to a loyalty signup. Capture email addresses and phone numbers before the guest walks out.

## 5. Takeout and delivery

Put a QR code on your storefront window pointing to your online ordering page. Passersby can place an order without stepping inside.

## The common thread

Every one of these uses a **dynamic QR code** — because the destination needs to change. Your menu changes, your promotion ends, your reservation link updates. With a dynamic QR, you update the destination on your dashboard and every printed code in your restaurant instantly points to the new page.
